# Bruises

## Immediate steps
- Elevate the bruised area above heart level if possible.
- Apply an ice pack wrapped in a thin towel for about 20 minutes at a time.
- Repeat cold application a few times during the first day or two to limit swelling.
- If swelling occurs, a light elastic bandage may help, as long as it is not wrapped tightly.

## Do not
- Do not apply ice directly to bare skin.
- Do not wrap an elastic bandage tightly enough to restrict circulation.
- Do not ignore bruising that appears suddenly or without any clear injury.

## Seek emergency help when
- The bruised area develops very painful swelling or a hard lump.
- Bruising occurs frequently, or involves unusually large or painful bruises, without a clear cause.
- There is unusual bleeding elsewhere (nose, gums) alongside the bruising.
- The bruising is associated with a significant injury or suspected fracture.
- Pain persists or worsens after what seemed like a minor injury.
