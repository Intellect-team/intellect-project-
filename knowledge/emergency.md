# General Emergency Guidance

## Immediate steps
- Stay calm and assess whether the scene is safe before approaching.
- If the person is unresponsive, check for normal breathing.
- Call local emergency services immediately for any life-threatening situation, then begin appropriate first aid while help is on the way.
- Keep the person as still and comfortable as possible until help arrives, unless remaining in place is dangerous.

## Do not
- Do not move a person with a suspected spinal, neck, or serious bone injury unless they are in immediate danger.
- Do not give food or water to someone who is unconscious, having trouble breathing, or about to receive surgery.
- Do not delay calling emergency services to attempt first aid first when the situation is clearly severe.

## Seek emergency help when
- The person is unconscious, unresponsive, or not breathing normally.
- There is uncontrolled bleeding, a suspected broken bone with deformity, or signs of shock.
- You are not confident about the severity of the situation — it is always safer to call and let professionals assess it.
