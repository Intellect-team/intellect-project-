# Choking

## Immediate steps
- Ask "Are you choking?" — if they can cough forcefully or speak, encourage them to keep coughing.
- If they cannot breathe, cough, or speak: give up to 5 sharp back blows between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand.
- If that does not clear it, give up to 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich maneuver): stand behind them, place a fist above the navel, and thrust inward and upward.
- Alternate 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts until the object clears or the person can breathe/cough/speak again.

## Do not
- Do not perform abdominal thrusts on infants under 1 year old — use back blows and chest thrusts instead.
- Do not attempt a blind finger sweep of the mouth unless you can clearly see and reach the object.
- Do not leave the person alone while they are still choking.

## Seek emergency help when
- The person becomes unconscious — call emergency services immediately and begin CPR if trained.
- The obstruction does not clear after several rounds of back blows and abdominal thrusts.
- The person is pregnant or too large to effectively encircle for abdominal thrusts (use chest thrusts instead).
