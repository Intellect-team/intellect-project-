# Burns

## Immediate steps
- Cool the burn under cool (not ice-cold) running water for 10-20 minutes.
- Remove nearby jewelry or tight clothing before swelling starts.
- Cover loosely with a clean, non-fluffy cloth or cling film.

## Do not
- Do not apply ice directly to a burn.
- Do not apply butter, oil, or toothpaste.
- Do not pop any blisters that form.

## Seek emergency help when
- The burn is larger than the person's hand.
- The burn is on the face, hands, joints, or genitals.
- The skin is blistered, white, charred, or leathery.
- The burn was caused by chemicals, electricity, or a large flame.
